# Measuring AI ROI & Business Impact

> **Track:** Anthropic Enterprise Implementation (Track 1)
> **Course 6 of 6**

Quantify the business value of AI implementations with measurement frameworks, KPIs, and executive reporting.

---

## Modules

### Module 1: Baseline Measurement Frameworks

**Learning Objectives:**
- Define pre-AI performance baselines
- Select comparable metrics
- Document measurement methodology

### Module 2: KPIs for AI Implementations

**Learning Objectives:**
- Identify leading and lagging indicators
- Set realistic improvement targets
- Build KPI tracking dashboards

### Module 3: A/B Testing AI Features

**Learning Objectives:**
- Design controlled AI experiments
- Calculate statistical significance
- Interpret and act on results

### Module 4: Executive Reporting & Business Cases

**Learning Objectives:**
- Structure ROI presentations for executives
- Quantify time and cost savings
- Build the case for AI budget expansion
